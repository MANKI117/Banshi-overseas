Visa Pathways & Document Guide

These are general starting points. Requirements differ by visa type, personal circumstances and current official rules.

🇨🇦 Canada

Possible pathways include study permits, employer-specific or other eligible work permits, and visitor visas.

Common document checklist

  • Valid passport and photographs, where required
  • Application forms and fee receipts
  • Study: acceptance letter and evidence of funds, as required
  • Work: valid job offer and required employer/permit documents, where applicable
  • Evidence of purpose of travel, ties and supporting funds, as applicable

Typical process outline

Check eligibility → prepare evidence → submit the correct application → provide biometrics/medical documents if requested → wait for an official decision.

A job offer or admission does not guarantee a permit or visa. Check current requirements with the official Canadian immigration authority.
Enquire about Canada

🇬🇧 United Kingdom

Options may include Student, Visitor and eligible sponsored work routes. Each route has its own criteria.

Common document checklist

  • Valid passport and application details
  • Study: confirmation from an eligible sponsor and financial evidence, as required
  • Work: eligible sponsored job and Certificate of Sponsorship, where required
  • Evidence of funds, travel purpose and accommodation, as applicable
  • Translations and other evidence if required

Typical process outline

Choose the correct route → check eligibility and documents → apply online → attend biometrics or other appointments if required → await the decision.

Work permission depends on the specific route and sponsorship rules. Verify current details on GOV.UK.
Enquire about UK

🇦🇺 Australia

Study, visitor and work-related visa options depend on the applicant and the specific visa subclass.

Common document checklist

  • Passport and identity documents
  • Study: enrolment confirmation and evidence required for the application
  • Work: documents relevant to the selected visa and any sponsorship/skills criteria
  • Financial, health, character and English evidence where required
  • Travel purpose and supporting documents

Typical process outline

Identify the right visa subclass → check current criteria → lodge through the official system → complete health/biometrics steps if requested → await a decision.

Eligibility, work rights and processing times vary. Check the Australian Department of Home Affairs website.
Enquire about Australia

🇳🇿 New Zealand

Study, visitor and work visa routes have different requirements; some work routes require an eligible job offer.

Common document checklist

  • Valid passport and identity documents
  • Study: offer of place and funds evidence, where required
  • Work: job offer/employer information and role-specific evidence, where required
  • Health, character, funds and genuine-intention evidence as applicable
  • Translations or additional documents requested by the authority

Typical process outline

Check the visa type → confirm eligibility → prepare documents → apply online → complete any requested checks → await the decision.

A job offer does not itself grant work rights. Check Immigration New Zealand's current rules.
Enquire about New Zealand

🇮🇪 Ireland

Employment permits, study permissions and visitor arrangements are separate processes with different conditions.

Common document checklist

  • Passport and application forms
  • Work: eligible job offer and the relevant employment permit documentation
  • Study: course/enrolment evidence and required financial documents
  • Qualifications, experience and translations, where relevant
  • Funds, travel purpose and other supporting evidence as applicable

Typical process outline

Check the job/course and eligibility → prepare the required permit or visa application → submit supporting evidence → complete any further steps requested.

Not every job qualifies for an employment permit. Verify current rules through Ireland's official government services.
Enquire about Ireland

🇫🇮 Finland

Long-stay study or work usually involves a residence-permit route; short visits follow separate rules.

Common document checklist

  • Valid passport and identity details
  • Study: admission/acceptance and funds/insurance evidence, as required
  • Work: employment terms and employer information required for the permit type
  • Qualifications, experience and supporting documents, if relevant
  • Application, identity verification and appointment documents

Typical process outline

Choose the correct permit → check eligibility and evidence → submit the application → verify identity as instructed → wait for the authority's decision.

Permit rules, salary criteria and rights vary by category. Check the Finnish Immigration Service (Migri).
Enquire about Finland

🇳🇴 Norway

Work and study residence permits depend on the category, qualifications and the applicant's circumstances.

Common document checklist

  • Valid passport and identity documents
  • Work: qualifying job offer and documents required for the selected category
  • Study: admission and proof of funds, as required
  • Qualifications, work experience and translations where relevant
  • Application, appointment and supporting evidence

Typical process outline

Identify the relevant permit → confirm eligibility → collect documents → apply using the official instructions → attend any required appointment → await the decision.

Job, wage and permit conditions depend on the applicable route. Check the Norwegian Directorate of Immigration (UDI).
Enquire about Norway

🇪🇺 Europe

Europe is not one single work or study visa area for every purpose. Requirements depend on the destination country and route.

Start with these documents

  • Valid passport and identity documents
  • Purpose of travel: study, work, visit or family route
  • Admission or eligible employment documents, if relevant
  • Funds, insurance, accommodation and other evidence as required
  • Country-specific forms and appointment requirements

Typical process outline

Choose the destination country → identify the official visa/permit category → check its requirements → apply through the authorised process → await a decision.

Visa rules are country-specific. Confirm the exact destination and route before preparing an application.
